## Brand & Style

The brand identity embodies a modern dark gourmet bistro aesthetic tailored for an artisanal burger and steak craft destination in Paraguay. It converges the raw energy of an open wood-and-charcoal flame with refined culinary craftsmanship. The visual direction targets discerning diners, food enthusiasts, and cocktail aficionados who seek an elevated, moody evening atmosphere without formal rigidity.

The design movement merges **Tactile Dark Minimalism** with **Selective Warm Glassmorphism**. Dark charcoal and smoldering graphite planes serve as an intimate backdrop, illuminated by focused amber-gold highlights and glowing embers of crimson. Surfaces evoke cast iron, aged oak, and brushed brass. Interfaces prioritize atmospheric depth, tactile micro-interactions, rich dish photography, and crisp editorial balance, projecting high culinary value and sensory indulgence.

## Colors

The palette creates an atmospheric, low-lit culinary dining environment punctuated by warm luminescence:

- **Primary (`#F59E0B`)**: Pure Amber Gold, used for high-tier focal points, rating stars, active badges, and key conversion paths (e.g., "Reservar Mesa", "Pedir Gourmet").
- **Secondary (`#DC2626`)**: Deep Flame Crimson, referencing searing charcoal flames and prime cuts. Applied to alert tags, artisanal sauce designations, fire-grilled indicators, and urgent notifications.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`)**: Burnished Ochre Brass, supporting primary accents across borders, hover states, sub-captions, and subtle glow treatments.
- **Neutral Tiers**:
  - `Base Canvas`: `#121214` (Deep Smoked Charcoal)
  - `Surface Tier 1 (Cards & Sidebars)`: `#1A1A1E` (Dark Slate Graphite)
  - `Surface Tier 2 (Elevated Sheets & Floating Nav)`: `#232329` (Muted Cast Iron)
  - `Surface Tier 3 (Input Fields & Segmented Controls)`: `#2C2C35`
- **Text & Accents**:
  - `Text Primary`: `#F4F4F6` (Pristine Warm White, 95% opacity)
  - `Text Secondary`: `#A1A1AA` (Ash Gray, 70% opacity)
  - `Border Subtle`: `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)` (Warm Gilded Stroke)
  - `Border Focus`: `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.45)`

## Typography

The typography system projects culinary authority, artisanal precision, and modern dining sophistication:

- **Headlines (`Syne`)**: Chosen for its sculpted, avant-garde character and wide stance. It delivers an unforgettable culinary presence in big titles, dish category names, and marquee headers.
- **Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers clear readability and balanced warmth for ingredient lists, tasting notes, and culinary narratives across screens.
- **Labels & Metrics (`Space Grotesk`)**: Technical, clean, and modern. Used uppercase for price tags, currency indicators (`Gs. / PYG`), dietary badges, cooking temps, and metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a fluid 12-column responsive grid on desktop with a fixed max-width container of `1280px` to maintain focused culinary storytelling.

- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Mobile`: `< 640px` (4-column layout, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`).
  - `Tablet`: `640px - 1024px` (8-column layout, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`).
  - `Desktop`: `> 1024px` (12-column layout, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`).

Spacing rhythm follows an 8pt base grid. Component cards, menu items, and editorial story sections leverage generous vertical padding (`space-xl`) to establish an open, high-end feel. Full-bleed hero photography transitions smoothly into grid-bound panels using deep gradient fades.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through **Tonal Layers** combined with **Frosted Dark Glass** and **Amber Ambient Glows**:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas)**: `#121214` solid background with faint radial dark gradients.
- **Layer 1 (Standard Card Surfaces)**: `#1A1A1E` at 90% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle `1px` border of `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`.
- **Layer 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Flyouts)**: `#232329` at 95% opacity with an ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 16px 0 rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08)`. Border shifts to `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Tasting Drawers, Cart Drawer)**: `#1A1A1E` backed by an overlay backdrop of `rgba(10, 10, 12, 0.8)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` and top-edge amber specular line: `linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4), transparent)`.
- **Accent Embers**: Highlight cards for special cuts and chef signatures utilize an inner border glow: `box-shadow: inset 0 0 20px rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements balanced `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem / 8px default radius, 1rem / 16px for cards, and 1.5rem / 24px for floating dialogs and sheets). 

This moderate curvature softens the technical precision of the dark interface without sliding into casual playfulness. Food image containers feature matching 16px internal corner radiuses to echo culinary plating lines. Badges, tags, and pills maintain full `9999px` circular capsules to offset rectilinear item cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Flame Gold)**: Solid `#F59E0B` fill with `#121214` typography (`label-lg`), 8px corner radius, and subtle amber glow (`0 4px 16px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`). On hover: `#D97706` fill with expanded glow.
- **Secondary Action (Charcoal Ghost)**: Transparent fill with `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)` border, `#F4F4F6` text. On hover: background shifts to `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08)` and border brightens to full `#F59E0B`.
- **Tertiary Action (Smoked Crimson)**: Background `rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.15)` with `#DC2626` text and border `rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.3)`, used for limited-time chef recommendations and special pairings.

### Cards (Menu Items & Burger Crafts)
- Dark glass surface (`#1A1A1E` at 85% opacity, `16px` backdrop blur).
- Outer perimeter: `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`.
- Top image container: 16:10 aspect ratio with smooth gradient shade into card body.
- Content stack: Chef signature badge (`label-sm`), item name (`title-lg`), ingredient breakdown (`body-sm` in `#A1A1AA`), and trailing bottom row containing formatted price (`title-md` in `#F59E0B`) and quick-add icon trigger.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- Capsule radius (`9999px`), `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- Smoked background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` border.
- Active category filter chip: `#F59E0B` text with `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)` background and `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)` border.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#1A1A1E`. Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Radius: `8px`.
- Input text: `#F4F4F6`, placeholder in `#71717A`.
- Active focus state: border shifts to `#F59E0B` with `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Base: `20px` square (or circle) with `#232329` fill and `1.5px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- Checked state: `#F59E0B` fill with `#121214` icon checkmark.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Doneness Selector**: Segmented horizontal stepper with charred color gradient (Bleu, Jugoso, A Punto, Cocido).
- **Paraguayan Craft Pairing Pill**: Small callout badge displaying craft beer or wine pairing suggestions with micro-bottle iconography and warm gilded highlights.
